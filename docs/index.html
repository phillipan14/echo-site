What Echo does

Six quiet jobs, one app. Each does enough on its own to be a product. Together they turn the months of meetings you'd otherwise forget into something you can actually use.

TRANSCRIBE

Live transcript, in any language.

Words appear as people talk. Apple Speech runs on-device — audio never leaves your iPhone. Multilingual mode handles code-switching between English and Mandarin in a single recording.

EXTRACT

People and action items, automatic.

The moment you hit End, Echo pulls out who was there and what needs doing. Review and accept in one tap — only checked items land in your network.

ASK

Chat with your meetings.

"What did Bob say about pricing?" Echo answers from the actual transcript — grounded in what was said, not invented. Works in any single meeting and across your full history.

COLLECT

Conferences and multi-day events.

Group sessions into a Collection and Echo synthesises across them — themes that emerged, decisions across days, follow-ups due. One view for a three-day summit.

JOURNAL

Not just meetings — voice memos, events, journals.

Four recording modes share the same engine. Speak a journal entry on a walk; Echo transcribes it and files it next to the meetings that prompted it.

INBOX

Every action item, one list.

Tasks from every meeting flow into a single Inbox. Cross-meeting, searchable, with follow-ups separated from to-dos. Open vs Done. Nothing slips.

LIVE TRANSCRIPTION

Captions as the room speaks — in 30+ languages, on-device.

Tap once. Echo starts listening, draws a waveform, and writes the transcript line by line as it hears it. Apple Speech runs locally on your iPhone — your audio never leaves the device, even when you turn the screen off.

The multilingual picker handles the awkward case nothing else does well: a meeting where speakers switch between English and Mandarin mid-sentence. Echo transcribes both in a single pass.

  • Switch language with one tap (EN, 中文, and more)
  • Live captions, bookmarks, and inline notes while recording
  • Keeps transcribing with phone locked or on a call
EXTRACT — PEOPLE & ACTIONS

People and to-dos, pulled out the moment you stop.

The instant you tap End, Echo reads back the transcript and surfaces what mattered: who was mentioned, who you met, and what was actually agreed. You see a small review screen — everything pre-checked — and you choose what lands in your network.

No more "I'll write up the action items later." They're already written. You just confirm.

  • People auto-recognised — names, roles, companies
  • Action items extracted from what was said, not inferred
  • One tap to add, one tap to skip
ASK THIS MEETING

Chat with the transcript, like the meeting is still in the room.

Every recording becomes a small searchable archive of itself. Tap Ask this meeting at the bottom of any note and ask anything — what was decided, what your name was attached to, what the speaker actually said about timelines.

Answers are grounded in the transcript, never invented. Bring your own AI key (Claude or DeepSeek) — only the text is sent, never the audio. Most meetings cost less than a cent to query.

  • Answers cite the moment in the transcript
  • Continue the conversation thread across follow-ups
  • Or ask the same question across every meeting at once
CONFERENCES & COLLECTIONS

A three-day summit, in one synthesised view.

Group recordings into a Conference and Echo treats the whole event as one thing. A multi-day programme becomes a single page: sessions in order, themes that emerged across days, the follow-ups owed back to other delegates.

Built for the kind of week where you do eight talks, four side meetings, and a panel — and lose track of which decision came from which room.

  • Date-bounded multi-day collections
  • Cross-session synthesis (themes, decisions, open threads)
  • Per-session transcripts and action items still intact
FOUR RECORDING MODES

Meetings, voice memos, events — and a journal mode for walks.

Echo isn't only for meetings. Before you hit record, pick a type: Meeting, Voice memo, Event, or Journal. The transcript engine is the same; the way Echo files and surfaces the recording is not.

Journal mode is the quiet one. Talk for two minutes on the walk home and Echo writes it down for you — searchable, dated, sat next to the meetings that prompted the thought.

  • Meeting — people, action items, summary
  • Voice memo — quick capture, no extractions
  • Event — bind to a calendar block
  • Journal — long-form personal notes, transcribed
INBOX

Every action item, one list, across every meeting.

The action items Echo extracts don't get stranded inside individual notes. They collect in your Inbox — a single to-do list spanning every meeting, voice memo, and journal entry.

Open and Done split. Follow-ups (things to send other people) separate from your own tasks. Sort by newest, by meeting, by date. Tap any item to jump back to the exact moment in the transcript where it came from.

  • Tasks and Follow-ups, separated
  • Cross-meeting, cross-language (中文 and English share one Inbox)
  • Each item traces back to the line that produced it
SUMMARIES & KEY POINTS

Notes that read like a magazine, not a wall of text.

Hit End and Echo writes the summary in seconds. Not a literal transcript dump — a clean editorial paragraph plus numbered key points, in the same serif that runs across the rest of the app.

Bring your own AI key (Claude or DeepSeek). You pay them directly, pennies per meeting. The transcript itself is what's sent — audio stays on your phone.

  • Editorial-style summary + numbered key points
  • Powered by Claude or DeepSeek (your key, your spend)
  • Re-run any time the model improves
TRANSCRIPT & MARKS

Every word, timestamped — and tap any line to play from it.

The full transcript is always one tap away. Each segment is timestamped, searchable, and lined up with the audio. Drop a bookmark mid-meeting and you can find the exact moment again in a week.

This is the source of truth behind every summary, every action item, and every answer Echo gives you.

  • Word counts, segment timestamps, full-text search
  • Play audio from any line
  • Bookmarks and photo marks taken while recording
PEOPLE

A network that builds itself, meeting by meeting.

Every person Echo extracts joins your People view. Roles, companies, last-met dates, and the action items still open with each of them — all stitched together without you typing a CRM entry.

Optional one-tap sync with iOS Contacts fills in emails, phones, and LinkedIns; Echo dedupes the duplicates so the same person doesn't show up three ways.

  • People auto-extracted from what was actually said
  • Last-met dates and per-person open tasks
  • Sync with iOS Contacts to fill in details (opt-in)

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your meetings stay yours.

Echo has no server. No account. No analytics. No third-party trackers. Audio recordings live in your iPhone's local storage. Transcripts stored in SwiftData on your device. AI features are opt-in — when you add your own API key, only the transcript text is sent, never the recording.

Questions

Is Echo free?

The app itself is free. AI features (summaries, the Ask-this-meeting chat) use your own API key from Claude or DeepSeek — you pay them directly, typically pennies per meeting. No subscription to Echo.

Does Echo work with phone locked?

Yes. Echo keeps transcribing even when you lock your phone or switch to another app. The recording survives interruptions like phone calls.

What languages does transcription support?

30+ languages via Apple Speech, on-device. Multilingual mode is built for code-switching speakers (e.g. an English-Mandarin meeting transcribes both languages in one pass).

Does Echo work for in-person meetings or just calls?

Both. Echo uses your iPhone's microphone, so it captures any audio your phone can hear — in-person rooms, Zoom on speaker, AirPods, etc.

Can I use Echo just for personal journals?

Yes. Journal is one of four recording modes and skips the people/action-item extraction by default. Speak; Echo transcribes; the entry files itself with a date and lives alongside your meetings (but private).

What's the difference between a Conference and a regular meeting?

Conferences are Collections — multi-day, date-bounded groupings of related recordings. You get one synthesised page across all the sessions: themes, decisions, follow-ups owed. Useful for summits, off-sites, or any week where ten meetings belong to one story.

Where do my notes live?

On your iPhone, in SwiftData. iCloud Backup catches them like any iPhone app. No server, no account — your meetings never leave your device unless you choose to send the transcript text to an AI provider for summary.

Who's behind Echo?
